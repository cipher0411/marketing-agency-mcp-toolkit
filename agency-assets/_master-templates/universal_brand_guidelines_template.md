# Universal Brand Guidelines Template

Copy into a client's `_context/client_brand_style_guide.md` and fill in per-client. This is the skeleton every client brand doc should follow so voice/visual QA checks stay consistent across accounts.

## 1. Brand essence
- Mission statement:
- One-sentence positioning:
- Brand personality (3–5 adjectives):
- What we are NOT (common mispositioning to avoid):

## 2. Visual identity
- Primary logo + usage rules (min size, clear space, don't-do list)
- Color palette: primary, secondary, accent — hex/RGB/CMYK
- Typography: headline font, body font, fallback web-safe stack
- Photography/illustration style (mood, do/don't examples)

## 3. Voice & tone
- Voice attributes (e.g. "confident but not arrogant")
- Tone shifts by channel (LinkedIn vs. support email vs. ad copy)
- Words/phrases to use vs. avoid
- Reading level target

## 4. Messaging
- Core value propositions (max 3)
- Proof points / stats to cite
- Tagline(s) and approved variations

## 5. Application examples
- Sample social post
- Sample email subject lines
- Sample ad headline + body

## 6. Approval chain
- Who signs off on brand-adjacent creative before it ships (name/role)
- Escalation path if a piece is time-sensitive and the approver is unavailable
