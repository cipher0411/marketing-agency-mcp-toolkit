# Universal SOP Template

Use this skeleton for every new SOP so process docs stay scannable and consistent agency-wide.

## SOP Title
**Owner:** (role responsible for keeping this current)
**Last reviewed:** (date)
**Applies to:** (which clients/teams/situations)

## Purpose
One or two sentences: what breaks if this SOP isn't followed?

## Trigger
What starts this process (a request type, a calendar event, a threshold being crossed)?

## Steps
1. Step — who does it, what tool/MCP server (if any) is used, expected output
2. Step — ...
3. Step — ...

## Inputs required
- List of documents/data/access needed before starting

## Outputs
- What gets produced, and where it's filed (which folder)

## Escalation
- When to stop and ask a human/senior before proceeding
- Who to escalate to

## Common failure modes
- What typically goes wrong and how to catch it early
