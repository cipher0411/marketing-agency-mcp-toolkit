# Quality Assurance SOP

**Owner:** Whoever is not the original author of the piece being reviewed
**Applies to:** Every deliverable before it ships to a client or goes live

## Checklist (all content types)
- [ ] Matches brand voice/style guide (`_context/client_brand_voice_guide.md`)
- [ ] Factually accurate — claims/stats have a source
- [ ] No unattributed copyrighted material reproduced (quotes under agency policy limits, properly attributed)
- [ ] Links work, CTAs point to the right destination
- [ ] Spelling/grammar pass
- [ ] Accessibility basics (alt text on images, sufficient color contrast for ad creative)

## AI-generated content specific
- [ ] Run through `quality_check` (Social Neuron) or manual review if that tool isn't enabled for this client
- [ ] Human reviewed for tone drift, hallucinated facts/stats, or generic phrasing that doesn't match brand voice
- [ ] Any AI-generated claim about the client's product/results is verified against the actual product/data, not assumed correct

## Sign-off
Nothing ships without a second person's sign-off logged (name + date) in the relevant project brief.
