# Crisis Communication SOP

**Owner:** Account lead + agency principal
**Applies to:** PR incidents, campaign backfires, data/security incidents, major platform outages affecting a client

## Trigger
Any of: negative viral moment involving client brand, ad campaign causing public backlash, a security/data incident (e.g. leaked API key, unauthorized account access), major outage of a tool the client depends on.

## Immediate steps (first hour)
1. Pause any scheduled/automated posts or campaigns for the affected client (`schedule_post` cancellations, pause ad spend) — stop the bleeding before drafting a response.
2. Notify account lead and agency principal directly, not just async.
3. Document what happened, when, and what's currently live/paused.

## Within 24 hours
4. Draft a response plan with the client — this agency does not publish a public statement on a client's behalf without their explicit sign-off.
5. If it's a security incident: rotate any exposed credentials immediately (`mcp-credentials-management.md`), assess what data/access was exposed, and inform the client in writing.

## After resolution
6. Post-mortem using `reports/campaign_post_mortem_template.md`.
7. Update relevant SOP if the incident revealed a process gap.

## Explicitly out of scope for autonomous action
No AI tool or automated workflow in this system publishes a crisis-related statement, pauses/resumes ad spend, or sends client-facing crisis communication without a human explicitly approving that specific action.
