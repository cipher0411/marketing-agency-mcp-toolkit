# Sales Deck Template (Client-Facing New Business)

1. Title slide (agency + prospect logo, only after prospect has agreed to be named)
2. Understanding of their challenge
3. Our approach
4. Relevant case studies (published/approved only)
5. Team
6. Scope & timeline
7. Investment
8. Why us / differentiation
9. Next steps

## Notes
Never include another client's confidential data/results in a prospect deck without that client's explicit permission — case studies used here must already be approved for external use.
