# Brand Approval SOP (Client)

## Approval tiers
| Content type | Approver | SLA |
|---|---|---|
| Social post (standard) | Internal QA only, per `_context/mcp_configuration.md` client tier | Same day |
| Blog/long-form | Client marketing contact | 2 business days |
| Paid ad creative | Client marketing contact | 2 business days |
| Anything mentioning specific claims/stats/pricing | Client + legal if regulated industry | 3 business days |
| Crisis-adjacent content | Agency principal + client | Immediate, see `crisis_communication_sop.md` |

## Process
1. Deliverable passes internal QA first (`agency-assets/_global-sops/quality_assurance_sop.md`).
2. Submit to client approver with clear deadline and one-line context.
3. Log approval (who, date) in the project brief.
4. If no response by SLA, follow up once, then escalate to account lead — don't publish on an assumed approval.
5. Nothing publishes, sends, or spends without a logged approval matching this table.
