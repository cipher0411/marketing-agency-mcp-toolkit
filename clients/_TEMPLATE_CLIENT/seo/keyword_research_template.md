# Keyword Research Template

| Keyword | Volume | Difficulty | Intent | Current ranking | Target page |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Research process
1. Seed keywords from `_context/client_product_offerings.md` and ICP pain points.
2. Expand via Ahrefs keyword research tools (`agency-assets/mcp-configurations/analytics-mcp-setup.md`).
3. Filter by intent (informational/commercial/transactional) and match to funnel stage.
4. Check competitor rankings (Site Explorer) for the same keywords — gauge realistic difficulty, not just the tool's score.

## Prioritization
Volume × relevance ÷ difficulty, weighted toward keywords matching high-intent funnel stages first.
