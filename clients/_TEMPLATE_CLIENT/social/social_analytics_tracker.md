## Data source
`fetch_analytics` / `get_performance_insights` (Social Neuron) — see `agency-assets/mcp-configurations/social-mcp-setup.md`.

## Review cadence
Weekly pull, monthly trend review feeding `reports/monthly_report_template.md`.

## What to watch for
Sudden drops usually mean a platform algorithm change or a tracking/API issue, not necessarily a content quality problem — check platform-wide benchmarks before concluding this client's content underperformed.
