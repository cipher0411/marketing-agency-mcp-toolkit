# Engagement Guide

## Response time targets
- Comments: within 24 hours
- DMs: within 24 hours (business hours)
- Negative comments/complaints: same day, escalate to account lead if it's a real grievance rather than routine feedback

## Tone
Match `_context/client_brand_voice_guide.md`; slightly warmer/more conversational than published content is typical for replies.

## Escalation
- Complaint about the product/service → log and route to client's support process, don't attempt to resolve product issues via social reply.
- Anything that could become a PR issue → `_global-sops/crisis_communication_sop.md`.
- Never argue publicly with a critic — acknowledge, take it to DM/support if resolution is needed.

## What NOT to do
No automated/AI-generated replies posted without review for a new client relationship — build trust in the tone match first before considering lighter-touch review.
