# Social Media Post Templates (Format Bank)

Reusable copy structures — pair with `_templates/social_media_post_template.md` for the per-post working doc.

## Educational/how-to
Hook (problem) → 3-5 step breakdown → CTA to learn more

## Behind-the-scenes
Authentic moment → why it matters → soft CTA (follow, engage)

## Customer proof
Quote/result → context → CTA to case study or product page

## Announcement
What's new → why it matters to the audience → CTA to act now

## Engagement/question
Genuine question tied to audience pain point → invite comments (don't fish for engagement with irrelevant questions)
