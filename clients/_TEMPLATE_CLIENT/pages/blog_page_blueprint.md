# Blog Page (Index) Blueprint

## Structure
- Featured/recent post
- Category filters (aligned to content pillars in `social/social_media_strategy_template.md` and SEO topic clusters in `seo/seo_strategy_template.md`)
- Search
- Pagination or infinite scroll (pick one, be consistent)

## SEO
- Category/tag pages should have unique meta descriptions, not duplicates
- Internal linking from index to individual posts

## Maintenance
Publishing cadence and who's responsible for keeping the index current (should update automatically if using client's CMS templating — confirm this rather than assuming).
