# About Us Page Blueprint

## Sections
1. Mission/founding story (authentic, not generic corporate speak)
2. Team (if client wants individual bios — get explicit consent per person before publishing photos/bios)
3. Values (3-5, each with a concrete example of how it shows up)
4. Milestones/timeline (if the client has a history worth telling)
5. CTA (careers, contact, or product depending on page goal)

## Voice notes
This page carries more personality than product pages — lean into `_context/client_brand_voice_guide.md`'s personality attributes.

## Facts to verify before publishing
Founding date, team headcount, any awards/certifications claimed — confirm current accuracy with the client, don't assume prior copy is still correct.
