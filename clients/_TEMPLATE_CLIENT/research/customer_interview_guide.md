# Customer Interview Guide

## Before the interview
- Get explicit consent to record/use quotes (see `_templates/case_study_template.md` approval requirements)
- Confirm the interviewee's role/relationship to the purchase decision

## Question bank
### Background
- Walk me through how you found [client]/discovered this problem.

### Problem
- What were you doing before? What wasn't working?

### Decision
- What almost stopped you from choosing [client]? What tipped it?

### Outcome
- What's changed since? (Push for specifics/numbers where possible)

### Close
- Would you be open to us quoting this / using it as a case study? (Get this explicitly, don't assume)

## After
File notes and any approved quotes for use in `_templates/case_study_template.md` and testimonials.
