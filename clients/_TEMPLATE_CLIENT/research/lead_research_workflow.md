# Lead Research Workflow (MCP)

Companion to `_sop/lead_generation_sop.md` — this doc covers the research/qualification side, not campaign execution.

## Company research
1. `get_company_info` / `company_dossier` (Forage) — firmographic snapshot.
2. Cross-reference against `_context/client_growth_marketing_context.md` ICP criteria before adding to a prospect list.

## Contact research
1. `find_leads` / `prospect_company` (Forage) or `search_people` (Apollo) — identify decision-makers.
2. `find_emails` / Hunter verification — confirm deliverable email before use.

## Qualification
Score against ICP fit (firmographic match, buying signals) before handing to outreach — don't push unqualified volume into `lead_generation_sop.md` execution just because sourcing was cheap.

## Cost awareness
Forage skills are billed per call (`company_dossier` $0.50, `prospect_company` $0.75, `outbound_list` $3.50/100) — batch research deliberately rather than querying one company at a time when a bulk skill exists.

## Output
Qualified list exported to Google Sheets or pushed to HubSpot, ready for `_sop/lead_generation_sop.md` execution.
